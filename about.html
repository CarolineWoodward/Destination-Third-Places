About the project

Why Destination: Third Places exists, and how each post gets made.

Thesis

Third places aren't defined by what they sell — they're defined by how visitors talk about them.

Some people believe that third places, or places other than home and work that serve as a gathering space for the community, are disappearing. It may or may not be true. But what is almost certainly true is that people are missing connections with their community. Destination: Third Places is searching for locations in Orange County that offer the main tenets of third places: a sense of belonging and comfort, accessible, and inexpensive.

The Capture Rule

Each day, find a location in Orange County, NY that could serve as a "third place" and capture evidence of its potential through their social media.

Third places must be low cost and convenient to come and go. They should encourage conversation and connections with people you might not know. Common third places include cafes, parks, libraries, gyms, and bars. A few of these common examples, like gyms and bars, can be more expensive than others, so discretion is required to select the ones that are most accessible. Spaces that cater to people with particular interests or have a degree of membership, like places of worship, Elks Clubs, or VFW posts, are eligible.

Digital spaces are able to serve as third places in a way, but since they lack face-to-face interactions and spontaneity, they don't fit the traditional idea of third places, and so are not eligible in this project. Third places can't be schools, homes, workplaces, or anywhere where strict productivity is expected, and lingering is discouraged.

One questionable situation would be about third places that don't meet at set locations. What if they move around from place to place? This may be the case for clubs like running or reading groups. They are certainly eligible as third places but will be difficult to represent within the limitations of my project. This is also the case for third places that don't have a strong social media presence and will unfortunately be overlooked.

Plan of Capture Action

For the sake of habit, I will go to a map of Orange County every day at 5 p.m. I will select a city or town in North Orange County the first day, East Orange County the next, South Orange County the next, and West Orange County the next, and then the cycle repeats. This will ensure an even representation across the county.

Within that city or town, I will find a third place and take a screenshot of the place on Google Street View to act as the main image for each post. I will also look through their Facebook or Instagram to screenshot evidence of its potential as a third place. I will annotate that screenshot with a note of what region of Orange County it falls in, a description of the place, and a few of its qualities as a third place. These qualities will become tags and allow a user to filter the list.

If I have trouble finding a place in a certain region, I will use Claude to give me recommendations. Since the days and times that I find captures don't affect the project's results, if I miss a day, I will make it up on a future date.

What This Should Do for a Reader

My audience are people within Orange County who feel like they are trapped in the home to work and work to home routine. They rarely go to a place where they can meet their neighbors and community and don't know where to start.

Upon finding my site, they should come away feeling empowered to find a third place of their own. There are places in their area they can easily visit and find the community they are lacking. It will be simple because they'll be able to organize these places by location and features. They don't need a ton of money, time, or commitment to enjoy these third places. They can just go.

Guardrails

The subjects of this project are places open to the public, frequently businesses. There should be no situation where I'd publish someone's private information or anything so personal that it would be inappropriate for my classmates to see.
