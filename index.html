Destination: Third Places

Third places aren't defined by what they sell — they're defined by how visitors talk about them.

Some people believe that third places, or places other than home and work that serve as a gathering space for the community, are disappearing. It may or may not be true. But what is almost certainly true is that people are missing connections with their community. Destination: Third Places is searching for locations in Orange County that offer the main tenets of third places: a sense of belonging and comfort, accessible, and inexpensive.
